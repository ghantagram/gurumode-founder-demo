How to explore

  1. 1

    Choose a simulation

    Pick a topic and select Explore to open its interactive model.

  2. 2

    Inspect a part

    Tap or click a part of the model, or choose it from the parts list.

  3. 3

    Change one setting

    Use the model’s settings to try a different value and see what changes.

  4. 4

    Watch and compare

    For moving models, press Play. Reset the model to try again and compare.
